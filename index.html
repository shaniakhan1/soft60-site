Before you begin

Questions about Soft60.

What is the Soft60 app?

Soft60 is a 60-day wellness practice for women, combining Morning Attunement, Today's Path, Restore, guided breathing, meditation, journaling, money exercises, live sessions and a private community.

How is Soft60 different from a habit tracker?

Instead of rewarding perfect streaks, Soft60 guides you through six daily laws and small, realistic choices. You can return when life gets messy without treating yourself as behind.

Is there a seven-day free trial?

Eligible new members can try Soft60 free for seven days. The app currently offers monthly and annual subscriptions at $19.99 and $149.99 respectively. Check the terms shown at checkout.

Is Soft60 available for iPhone and Android?

Yes, download Soft60 from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Members can also access the web experience.

Does Soft60 include breathing exercises and guided meditation?

Yes. Restore and private audio practices offer breathwork, meditation, and reflection. Soft60 is for personal development and wellness, not medical care.
