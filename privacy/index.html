Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Soft60 Daily is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Soft60 Daily application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Authentication data: information needed to securely sign you in.
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe or the applicable app store. We do not store your full card number.
  • User content: community posts, abundance entries, reflection notes, photos, and other content you choose to save or share.
  • Usage and device data: features accessed, session and interaction information, device type, operating system, and browser type.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to provide and operate Soft60, process subscriptions, send notifications with your permission, respond to support requests, improve the product, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Google Calendar Data

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Soft60 receives read-only access to upcoming events on your primary calendar. We access only the event ID, title, start and end date or time, location, free or busy status, all-day status, and event status. We retrieve up to 50 events from the next seven days. Soft60 cannot create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

We store encrypted Google access and refresh tokens and a server-side cache of those event fields so Schedule and Concierge features, including Build My Day, Should I Go, and Prioritize, can take your schedule into account. Calendar information included in a Concierge request may be processed by contracted AI and infrastructure providers solely to provide those features. Providers must protect the information and may not use it for independent advertising or unrelated purposes.

Calendar tokens are encrypted at the application level at rest using a separately managed server key. Calendar information is protected in transit using TLS/HTTPS and by access controls. Tokens and cached events are retained while Calendar remains connected. Disconnecting attempts to revoke Google authorization and immediately deletes locally stored tokens and cached events even if Google revocation fails. Permanent account deletion also deletes them. You may disconnect at any time and may additionally withdraw access through your Google Account permissions.

Soft60's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it except with your affirmative agreement for support or security, when required by law, or when necessary for approved internal security and operational purposes.

5. Service Providers

We share limited information with trusted providers that support authentication, payment processing, analytics, hosting, AI features, communications, and app operations. They are permitted to process information only to provide contracted services and must protect it.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@frequencyplanner.com.

California residents: You may request to know, correct, or delete personal information and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information.

European users: Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and may complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal information within 30 days except where retention is required by law, including financial records that may be retained for seven years.

8. Security

We use safeguards including TLS/HTTPS encryption for data in transit, application-level encryption for Google Calendar tokens, and access controls. Payment details are processed by payment providers and are not stored on our servers. No electronic storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Soft60 is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. Contact us if you believe such information was collected.

10. Push Notifications

With your permission, Soft60 may send reminders and other notifications. You can change notification preferences in the app or through your device settings.

11. Community Content and Moderation

Soft60 may offer community spaces where members post content. Members can report inappropriate content and block other users. We may review and remove content or suspend accounts that violate our Terms. Moderation records are used to enforce community standards.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted in Soft60 or otherwise communicated where required.

13. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to hello@frequencyplanner.com.
