How 4-7-8 breathing works

One complete breath has three parts.

4 · Inhale

Inhale gently through your nose for four counts.

7 · Hold

Pause for seven counts only if it's comfortable.

8 · Exhale

Exhale slowly and comfortably for eight counts.

The pattern is commonly called 4-7-8 breathing. Start with a small number of rounds rather than forcing a long session. The American Heart Association recommends stopping breathing exercises if you feel light-headed and consulting a clinician if you have a heart or lung condition before trying these techniques.

If breath-holding doesn't feel good

Try a softer alternative.

Inhale gently for four comfortable counts, then exhale for six. Repeat at a natural pace without holding your breath. If counting adds pressure, stop counting and return to your ordinary breathing.
