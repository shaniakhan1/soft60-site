What is a 60-day reset?

Sixty days of small decisions, rather than sixty days of proving yourself.

A 60-day reset is an intentional window to change how you move through ordinary life. In Soft60, the framework gives you a place to check in with your energy, choose a practical daily move, and reflect on what happened. There is no promise that every habit is fully formed on day 60. The point is to build a relationship with the practice that can continue afterward.

Research on habit formation finds wide variation in how long behavior becomes automatic. In one often-cited study, estimates ranged from 18 to 254 days, and missing one opportunity did not materially disrupt the process. That is one reason Soft60 emphasizes returning rather than punishing yourself for an imperfect streak. Read the study by Lally and colleagues.

How it works

Three moments. One daily path.

Morning Attunement

Begin by noticing whether you feel still, activated, heavy or aligned. There is nothing to perform here.

Your Daily Edit

Choose a meaningful next action. Make one thoughtful decision rather than collecting another overwhelming list.

Evening Reflection

Notice what worked, what you would do differently and what you want to remember about the day.

The six laws

The daily practices behind the reset.

Soft60's six practices ask you to regulate daily, subtract chaos, move gently, eat with presence, record soft identity proof, and reflect on your day. These are directions to return to, not boxes that determine your worth.

The most useful place to begin

Ask yourself: what one source of chaos am I participating in that I could reduce today? It may be a needless obligation, a phone notification, an argument you keep reopening, or a decision waiting for you. Choose one specific move. In Soft60, that decision becomes part of your daily path.

Questions

What if I miss a day?

Return. This is a practice, not a punishment. Missing a day does not erase your decisions, reflection or progress.

Do I have to finish everything?

Soft60 is designed around realistic daily choices. You can explore deeper experiences when they're useful, but the path starts with a manageable move.

Is Soft60 a therapy or medical app?

No. Soft60 is for wellness and personal development. Its reflections and exercises do not replace care from a qualified professional.
